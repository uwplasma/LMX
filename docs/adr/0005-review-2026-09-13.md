# 0005 — Decisions from the 2026-09-13 review

Status: accepted; supersedes the G4/G5 wording of 2026-09-07 and refines D2, D4, D5, D6 and the Phase 4 gates.

Basis: the measurements in `plan.md` Section 2 (state on 2026-09-13) and the
sources in Section 3.6. Each decision names the measurement that forced it.

**D15. Float32 GPU numbers are quoted only with TensorFloat-32 disabled.** JAX's
default matmul precision uses TF32 on Ampere; an A4000 float32 contraction
then differs from float64 by 3e-4 (2.6–6.1e-7 with `jax_default_matmul_precision`
set to `float32`). The package pins the precision when it enables a dtype, the
benchmark harness records it in every JSON, and float32 evidence without that
record is not quoted. The "float64 is 1/64 of float32" sentence is retracted:
measured, true-float32 contractions beat float64 by 0.9× at 64³, 4.1× at 128³
and 12× at 192³.

**D16. Mixed precision by defect correction, through SOLVAX.** The fast
diagonalization solves in float32 with float64 residual corrections
(`solvax.iterative_refinement` around `solvax.as_low_precision`). One
correction returns float64 accuracy (relative 1e-11) on wall-resolving meshes
up to a 2,831:1 width ratio, because the orthogonal transforms keep relative
accuracy mode by mode. This is the D4 promise, implemented rather than restated.

**D17. Krylov work in the steady path has O(1) memory.** The tangent and
transpose solves use the primal preconditioner (the unpreconditioned solve
fails at Ha 300 while the primal converges). The Stokes-limit steady operator
is symmetric negative definite in the face-volume inner product on uniform
meshes (asymmetry 2e-15), so preconditioned CG applies there; on stretched
meshes the electromotive and force interpolations are made discrete adjoints
of each other (asymmetry today 3.7e-2, the same defect as the first-order
ohmic identity), after which CG applies everywhere and the GMRES(400) basis --
20 GB at 128³ -- is not needed. Advective cases use recycled FGMRES
(`solvax.gcrot`) with restart ≤ 60.

**D18. The thin wall is a separable boundary row, not an inner Krylov solve.**
Krasnov, Akhtari, Zikanov & Schumacher (JCP 474:111784, 2023) fold the Walker
condition into the one-dimensional operators, so the potential solve stays
direct; where a wall is normal to a transformed axis, the tangential rows are
scaled by `h₁/(h₁ − 2c_w)` before the eigendecomposition. The perturbation
LMX's closure adds to the separable operator has rank 2·n_y (46 of 576
unknowns at 24²) and a Woodbury correction of the fast solve reproduces the
Krylov route to 8e-12, so either form replaces the inner GMRES. The wall
potential becomes its own unknown with the symmetric half-cell flux
`σ(φ_P − φ_w)/(h_P/2)`, a five-point surface Laplacian and conservative corner
cells: the present closure is first order and its operator asymmetric (0.27).

**D19. Reporting through the affine solve; the pseudo-time loop is a transient.**
The fully developed reporting loop stalls at an update of 1e-9 independent of
the time step, and more steps make it worse (2.9e-9 at 400 steps against
1.4e-9 at 96); its field differs from the direct affine solve of the same
linear problem by 1e-8. The affine GMRES already in `cases.py` certifies the
solution; #113 closes on that route, not on a longer campaign.

**D20. B1/B2 gates in tiers; a reduced model is the workhorse.** Hua et al.
(1988) is an inertialess SOR solution stopped at 0.1 %, ALEX walls are
`t_w/L ≈ 0.15`, the transverse pressure tap runs through the side wall, and
ALEX reported side-wall instabilities at B2's Reynolds number. A fully
resolved steady B2 is therefore neither affordable on 16 GB nor well posed
at 1e-3. Gates: ANL fringe within 1 % (code to code); B2 axial gradient within
5–10 % of experiment through the inertialess core-flow model with the measured
field and the tap correction; transverse Δp by shape and sign; the 3-D core
as a cross-check with continuation in the interaction parameter, fitted as
`Δp∞ + αHa^−1/2 + βN^−1/3`. The core-flow model replaces the benchmark-specific
lane as the general fringing-field screening tool.

**D21. Critical path first.** Until the fully developed cases run on the new
core and the B1/B2 and extruded lanes are deleted (steps 1.10b and 4.6), at most one open
pull request works outside Phases 1, 2 and 4, and no feature is added to the
older `cases.py`/`solvers.py` fully developed stack. Performance work names the profiled share of
runtime it targets (≥ 10 %) and the measured change; description length is
one page.
