# Code of Conduct

LMX is committed to a professional, inclusive, and evidence-focused research
community.

Participants must:

- communicate respectfully and critique ideas, code, and evidence rather than
  people;
- welcome questions and differing levels of experience;
- give credit for intellectual, software, data, and review contributions;
- disclose relevant uncertainty, limitations, and conflicts of interest;
- avoid harassment, discrimination, intimidation, sexualized attention,
  doxxing, and other conduct that makes participation unsafe.

This policy applies in the repository, project communication channels, meetings,
conferences, and other settings where someone represents LMX.

Report unacceptable behavior privately to the repository maintainers. Do not put
sensitive personal information in a public issue. Maintainers may warn, remove
content, restrict participation, or ban a participant, proportionate to the
conduct and with attention to the safety and privacy of affected people.

Good-faith scientific disagreement, a failed benchmark, or a rejected change is
not misconduct. Retaliation against someone who reports a concern is misconduct.
